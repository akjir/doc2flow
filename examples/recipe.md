# Recipe Overview

This recipe produces a rich, moist two-layer chocolate fudge cake topped with silky chocolate ganache frosting.

> Note: Make sure all chilled ingredients (eggs, milk, butter) are brought to room temperature before mixing.

## Ingredients & Quantities

The table below lists all required ingredients split by dry and wet components.

| Component | Ingredient | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dry | All-Purpose Flour | 250 g | Sifted |
| Dry | Granulated Sugar | 300 g | Fine grain |
| Dry | Unsweetened Cocoa Powder | 75 g | Dutch-processed |
| Dry | Baking Powder | 2 tsp | Fresh |
| Dry | Baking Soda | 1.5 tsp | Fresh |
| Dry | Fine Sea Salt | 1 tsp | - |
| Wet | Large Eggs | 2 pcs | Room temperature |
| Wet | Whole Milk | 240 ml | Room temperature |
| Wet | Vegetable Oil | 120 ml | Neutral flavor |
| Wet | Pure Vanilla Extract | 2 tsp | Natural |
| Wet | Boiling Water | 240 ml | Enhances cocoa flavor |

## Baking & Preparation Timings

Follow these timing parameters and temperature settings for accurate baking.

| Stage | Target Duration | Temperature | Equipment / Setup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prep & Measuring | 15 minutes | Room Temp (20°C) | Mixing bowls & whisk |
| Oven Preheating | 10 minutes | 175°C (350°F) | Conventional oven |
| Baking | 30 - 35 minutes | 175°C (350°F) | Two 9-inch round pans |
| Pan Cooling | 10 minutes | Room Temp | Wire cooling rack |
| Wire Rack Cooling | 45 minutes | Room Temp | Completely cooled |
| Frosting & Assembly | 15 minutes | Room Temp | Offset spatula |
